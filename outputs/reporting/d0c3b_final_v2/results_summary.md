### Main result

WT was the predeclared primary endpoint. All 270/270 WT cases were evaluable. Median case-level AUROC was 0.614130 [0.606392, 0.621043]; median AUPRC was 0.128997 [0.109080, 0.143982]; median AUPRC lift was 0.030396 [0.026699, 0.036569]; and median case-level DeltaZ difference was 0.010929 [0.010256, 0.011505]. 252/270 (93.3%) evaluable WT cases had a positive median difference. Holm-adjusted p-values were 6.062e-41 for AUROC versus 0.5, 2.604e-43 for AUPRC lift versus zero, and 2.261e-35 for median DeltaZ difference versus zero.

### Secondary results

TC was evaluable in 200/270 cases; its median AUROC was 0.619183, median AUPRC lift 0.009065, and median DeltaZ difference 0.010578. ET was evaluable in 198/270 cases; its median AUROC was 0.619359, median AUPRC lift 0.008520, and median DeltaZ difference 0.010651. TC and ET had fewer evaluable cases than WT.

### Interpretation

The results indicate a modest but consistent spatial association between the diffusion-derived deviation feature and tumor-containing regions. This association analysis is not evidence of diagnostic performance.

### Limitations

- The cohort is development-validation, not an independent test set.
- This is an association analysis, not a diagnostic evaluation; AUROC was moderate.
- Za is an anatomy/background representation and is not claimed to be healthy or pathology-free.
- DeltaZ has not been shown to improve a supervised segmentation model.
- Sensitivity timesteps 327 and 663 are deferred experiments.
