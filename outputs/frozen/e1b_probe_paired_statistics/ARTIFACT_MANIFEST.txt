Scientific interpretation:
Under identical E1-B latent-probe conditions, joint reconstruction did not
induce the desired preferential accessibility of pathology information from
Z_d. No evaluated region showed a statistically significant Z_d advantage
after Holm correction.

For SNFH and WT, Z_a instead showed significantly higher probe performance
than Z_d after Holm correction.

This result supports the narrower conclusion that joint reconstruction alone
was insufficient to induce the desired latent specialization. It does not
demonstrate or disprove semantic disentanglement in a general sense.

Cross-experiment caution:
The E1-B Z_a and Z_d probes use the same batch size (4), so their within-E1-B
paired comparison is controlled under the probe protocol. Direct numerical
comparison of E1-A and E1-B probe performance magnitudes is confounded by the
different probe batch size used for E1-A.
